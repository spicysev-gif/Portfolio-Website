MUREX Underwater Robotics ROV

An open-source underwater ROV designed for the 2024 MATE ROV competition. Built around a modular 6-DOF chassis, 7-DOF arm, and a custom electrical stack that undercuts commercial costs.

Overview

I led the design of a fully open-source underwater ROV intended to be small, low-cost, and modular without sacrificing capability. We focused on doing it right: open tooling, validated subsystems, and repeatable performance under competition conditions.

Highlights

  • 6-DOF frame with 7-DOF manipulator arm and modular mounts.
  • Custom PCBs for control, power, and sensor fusion.
  • Multi-camera vision with IP + USB streams.
  • Open-source stack that undercuts commercial costs by 88%.
  • Broke 10 industry records and placed 5th at Worlds.

Systems approach

Mechanical, electrical, and software teams worked in parallel. I standardized interfaces and enclosure dimensions so each subsystem could be developed asynchronously and still assemble cleanly.

  • Standardized connectors across boards and harnesses.
  • Mechanical frame designed for rapid swaps and upgrades.
  • Control stack tuned for stable 6-DoF motion.

What I built

  • Carrier board with USB 3.0 support and 10-DoF sensing.
  • Power board with monitoring and protection features.
  • ANYESC motion system for modular thruster control.
  • Ethernet switch for camera and control aggregation.
  • Topside system wiring and tether strain relief design.
